Privacy Policy - Rome Strategy War

Pagina Blanca Tech. Effective date: October 12, 2026.

Summary

Rome Strategy War ("the Game") is published by Pagina Blanca Tech ("we", "us"). The Game does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Everything the Game stores stays on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address
  • No account or sign-in information (the Game has no accounts)
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics, tracking, or crash reports
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone

Data Stored on Your Device

To let you continue your campaign, the Game stores the following locally on your device, using the app's local storage:

  • Saved campaign progress (including the camera position)
  • Game settings (for example audio and camera options)
  • Hall of Fame records (faction name, score, number of days, and similar campaign statistics)
  • Small interface counters, for example which loading tips were shown

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear the app's data in your device settings.

Internet Use

The Game is designed to work offline, and all of its assets are included in the app. The Game does not send data to our servers or to third parties. Links in the Credits screen (for example, links to asset creators) open in your device's browser. Those websites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Contacting Us

If you choose to email us with feedback or a bug report, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and to improve the Game, and we do not share it with others. Email is handled by our email provider (Google Gmail).

Third-Party Software and Assets

The Game includes open-source libraries and licensed assets, which are listed on the Credits screen. They run entirely within the app and do not collect or transmit data on our behalf.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the Game collects no personal data, there is no child data to collect, store, or delete.

Your Choices

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. To remove the data stored on your device, uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your Android settings.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how the Game handles data (for example, if we add advertising or in-app purchases in a future version), we will update this policy and its effective date before that change is released.
